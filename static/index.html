Ethical Governance & Deployment Standards

Directly Addressing Section 14 Requirements

14.1 Informed Consent Framework

Voice cloning requires unambiguous, voluntary consent. This project trains only on the public VCTK research corpus with recorded consent. When importing external audio, user verification guarantees no non-consensual voice recreation.

14.2 Misuse Mitigation & Provenance

To thwart deepfakes and impersonation, all waveforms synthesized by our pipeline inject:

  • Inaudible High-Frequency Carrier: A sub-perceptual tone at ~10.2 kHz that withstands acoustic re-encoding.
  • Cryptographic Header Tag: Embedded RIFF signature containing timestamp, speaker token, and model integrity hash.
  • Session Rate Limiting: Enforces generation quotas to prevent automated voice harvesting.

14.3 Accessibility Impact

The principal motivation of this research is restorative communication: enabling individuals affected by ALS, stroke, or laryngectomy to reclaim a natural, personalized voice identity rather than generic synthetic output.
