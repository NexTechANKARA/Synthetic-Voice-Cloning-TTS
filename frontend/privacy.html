Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026.

The short version

The text you type, the voice you record or upload, and the audio Voice Cloner generates are processed on your own device, in your browser. None of that is sent to a server or stored anywhere by us.

What we collect, and why

Account details

To sign in, we use Supabase Authentication. If you sign in with email and password, Supabase stores your email address and a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google, Supabase stores the email address and name Google shares with us, and we never see your Google password. This is only used to know it's you and to keep you signed in.

Your voice and your text

Everything the Studio does — reading your text, recording or reading an uploaded clip of your voice, and generating speech — runs with on-device machine learning models inside your browser. None of it is uploaded, logged, or stored by us, and it disappears when you close or refresh the page.

Local device storage

Your browser keeps your signed-in session and your light/dark theme choice in local storage on your own device, so you don't have to sign in or reset the theme every visit. This never leaves your device.

Payment information

We don't collect any. Voice Cloner has no paid tier and never asks for card details.

Who else is involved

Voice Cloner is hosted on Netlify and uses Supabase for sign-in, and sign-in with Google uses Google's own sign-in service. Each of these only receives the account information needed to sign you in or serve the page — never your text, your recordings, or generated audio.

Your choices

You can use the Model results page without an account at all. If you have an account and want it removed, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository and we'll delete your account details from Supabase.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect ever changes, this page will change with it. There's nothing to sign up for — just check back here.
