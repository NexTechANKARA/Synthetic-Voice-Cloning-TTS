Using Voice Cloner

Voice Cloner is a free, independent project that turns typed text into speech, either in one of 80 built-in voices or in a voice you record or upload yourself. By creating an account or using the Studio, you agree to these terms.

Your voice and the voices you use

If you add your own voice, you're confirming it's your voice, or that you have permission from the person speaking — the same thing the consent checkbox in the Studio asks you to confirm. Don't record or upload someone else's voice without their permission, and don't use Voice Cloner to impersonate a real person, mislead anyone, or generate speech that could cause harm.

No account fees, no guarantees

Voice Cloner is provided as-is, free of charge, with no guarantee it will always be available or error-free. The built-in voices and your own generated audio are yours to use; we don't claim ownership over anything you create with it.
